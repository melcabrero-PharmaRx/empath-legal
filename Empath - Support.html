Empath - Support

Empath is a calm, private AI companion that listens, offers gentle support, and helps you remember what matters with reminders for medication, appointments, and birthdays.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account? No. Empath works without any sign-up.

Where are my reminders stored? Entirely on your device. They are never uploaded.

Why don't reminder alerts fire? Make sure you allowed notification permission for Empath in your device settings.

Is Empath a replacement for therapy? No. Empath is an AI companion, not a licensed therapist or medical service. In a crisis, call or text 988 (US) or your local emergency number.
