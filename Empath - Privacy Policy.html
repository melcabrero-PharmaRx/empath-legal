Privacy Policy for Empath

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Empath ("the app", "we", "us") is an AI emotional-support companion. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. We built Empath to be private by design.

No account required

Empath does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number to use the app.

Reminders stay on your device

When you create reminders (for medication, appointments, birthdays, or other events), the reminder title, type, date/time, repeat setting, and any notes you add are stored only on your device. This information is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and it is removed when you delete the reminder or uninstall the app. Reminder alerts are scheduled locally by your device.

Chatting with the AI companion

When you choose to talk with the AI companion, the messages you send are transmitted to our AI service provider in order to generate a response for you. These messages are processed to provide the conversation feature. We do not use your conversations to build an advertising profile, we do not sell them, and because the app has no account, they are not linked to your real-world identity by the app.

Please avoid sharing information you would not want processed by an AI service (for example, passwords or financial account numbers).

Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or calendar.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or rent any personal information.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the app schedules local reminder notifications on your device. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.

Not a medical or crisis service

Empath is an AI companion and is not a licensed therapist or a medical service. It is not intended for emergencies. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (in the US) or your local emergency number.

Children

Empath is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
